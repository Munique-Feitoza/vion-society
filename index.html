Practical Intelligence

Know Before You Depart

Essential curated guidance for smooth arrivals and culturally attuned expeditions.

Visa & ETA Entry Requirements

Most foreign nationals require an electronic travel authorization (ETA) prior to arrival. Passports must be valid for 6 months. VION's concierge team assists all booked guests with express VIP arrival coordination at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB).

The Dual Monsoon Secret (Year-Round Sun)

Sri Lanka is a year-round destination thanks to its contrasting weather systems. When it rains in the southwest (May to September), the eastern beaches (Arugam Bay, Passikudah) enjoy pristine calm waters and blue skies.

Currency & Zero-Surcharge Billing

The local currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). Major international credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) are accepted at all luxury properties. All VION quotes and contracts are settled securely in USD/EUR/GBP with zero surprise exchange surcharges.

Private Chauffeur & Road Protocol

Due to winding hillside roads and diverse wildlife corridors, self-driving is discouraged. All VION journeys include an elite English-speaking chauffeur-guide in a luxury air-conditioned SUV with onboard high-speed Wi-Fi and chilled refreshments.
